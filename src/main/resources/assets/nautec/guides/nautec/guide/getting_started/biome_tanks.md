# <Color id="light_purple">Biome Tanks</Color>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glow_polyp_biome_tank" scale="2"/>
  <ItemImage id="kelp_biome_tank" scale="2"/>
  <ItemImage id="cactus_biome_tank" scale="2"/>
</Row>

A sealed glass tank holding a small piece of a biome. Each tank grows one plant on its own, with no power, water or light, so you can farm plants from biomes you are not in.

A tank grows one of its plant every 30 seconds (`biomeTankTicks` in `config/nautec-common.toml`) and holds up to a stack. Right-click it with an empty hand to take what it has grown, or pull it out with a hopper or pipe from any face. Nothing can be put in. When the stack is full the tank stops until you empty it.

***

## <Color id="gold">Crafting</Color>

Every tank uses the same recipe: 2 <ItemLink id="deep_steel_plating"/>, 5 Glass Panes, 1 Obsidian, and one of the plant it will grow in the middle.
