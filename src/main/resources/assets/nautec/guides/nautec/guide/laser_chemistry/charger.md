# <Color id="light_purple">Charger</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="charger" scale="2"/>
  Fills items that run on AP from a laser beam.
</Column>

## <Color id="gold">Using It</Color>

Right-click the Charger with an item to set it on the pad, and right-click with an empty hand to take it back. It holds one item at a time.

Aim a beam into any face. Any amount of power works, and the item fills at 4 AP per tick however strong the beam is. Sparks fly while it charges and stop when the item is full.

It charges any item that stores AP, including the [Aquarine Steel tools](tools.md) and armour, the [Prismatic Battery](prismatic_battery.md), the <ItemLink id="prism_monocle"/>, the <ItemLink id="wave_jet"/> and more.

| Item | Capacity | Time from empty |
|---|---|---|
| Aquarine Steel Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel or Sword | 1,200 AP | 15 seconds |
| Aquarine Steel Hoe | 700 AP | about 9 seconds |
| Prismatic Battery | 10,000 AP | about 2 minutes |
