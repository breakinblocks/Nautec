<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>

  The Wrench sets up and turns NauTec blocks, and the Crowbar opens Crates.
</Column>

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aquarine_steel_wrench"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aquarine Steel Wrench</Color>
</Row>

Right-click a block with the wrench:

* Any block with a facing, such as the <ItemLink id="aquatic_catalyst"/>, a relay or a machine, turns to its next direction. Keep clicking to go all the way round.
* An unformed Augmentation Station, Bio Reactor or Deep Sea Drain forms its multiblock when you click the controller block.
* A <ItemLink id="laser_junction"/> face becomes an input with a right-click and an output with a sneak right-click. Clicking a face again the same way turns it off.
* A wall block of a formed [Bio Reactor](nautec:aquatic_biology/bio_reactor.md) or [Deep Sea Drain](nautec:laser_chemistry/drain.md) opens a laser port there, so a beam can feed the machine. A Bio Reactor port closes again with another click.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="crowbar"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crowbar</Color>
</Row>

Right-click a closed Crate with the crowbar to pry it open. It has a short cooldown between uses. A Rusty Crate has to be etched into a Crate first (see [Crates and Etching](etching.md)).

The crowbar is made from Cast Iron Rods. Cast Iron comes from smelting Cast Iron Compound.
