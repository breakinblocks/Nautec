### Fixed
- Server lag when hundreds or thousand+ NauTec generators, the Fusion Controller, Extradimensional Storage output ports, Resonance Nodes or Prismatic Emitters sit next to machines or cables that are already full. Power output that moves nothing is now retried less and less often, down to once a second, and goes back to every tick as soon as power moves again.
- Potential Server lag when generators from other mods push power into a Conduit Tap whose conduit network is full.
