### Added

- Added the Crystal Cultivator to accelerate budding crystals and harvest them with laser-powered Fortune, including GeoOre and AE2 support.
- Added consumable Mob Samplers for retargeting Confined Spawners, including modded mobs and capture-tag restrictions.

### Fixed

- Fixed Bio Reactor tooltips reporting starvation when there is insufficient AP; they now show received and required power.
- Fixed machine recipes missing from the in-game guide, including ingredient counts, fluids and processing requirements.
- Fixed the Creative Flight augment losing flight permission while still installed.
- Fixed removing the Creative Flight augment disabling flight in Creative or Spectator mode.
- Fixed seams in flowing Electrolyte Algae Serum, Experience Algae and Etching Acid textures.
