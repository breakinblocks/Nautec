### Changed

- The Crystal Cultivator grows budding crystals 2.5 times faster at every power level. A 20 AP beam now gives 2.5x growth and 100 AP gives 25x.

### Fixed

- Fixed Vent Crawlers almost never spawning in Hydrothermal Vents. Squid and other sea life in the surrounding ocean no longer take up their spawn slots.
- Fixed Lantern Jellies spawning far less often than intended in the Bioluminescent Grove, for the same reason.
- Fixed an empty Sea Scout floating up off its Sea Scout Dock and drifting away. A powered dock now pulls an empty hull in and clamps it onto the pad, and a pilot can still drive straight off. (#43)
