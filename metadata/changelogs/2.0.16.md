### Added
- New Abyssal Enchanter: a 3x3 machine, two blocks tall, that puts the enchantment and level you pick on a book or item, using a reagent, liquid experience and a laser beam. Higher levels and rarer enchantments need a much stronger beam and more energy. Below purity 3.0 the result can drift, and below 0.5 it can pick up a curse. Every recipe is a datapack recipe, with extra ones for enchantments from Apothic Enchanting, EvilCraft, Mystical Agriculture, Reliquary, Occultism and others when they are installed, and JEI and EMI have a Laser Enchanting category. Enchantments can be kept out of it with the abyssalEnchanterBlacklist config option or the nautec:abyssal_enchanter_blacklist enchantment tag, and Ars Elemental's Soulbound is blocked by default. It can also overenchant items (not books) up to five levels past an enchantment's normal max, with each extra level costing four times the total energy and experience of the one before; the abyssalEnchanterOverenchant config option turns this off.
