### Added

- Added a Toggle Magnet Augments keybind (unbound by default).
- The Augmentation Station can now remove augments and returns the part. Empty the extensions, pick the augment and press Remove.
- Added the Bacterial Enrichment Catalyst, which raises a cultivated Prismarine Crystal up to purity 12, and the Prismatophiles strain it runs on.

### Changed

- Fusion Plant injector beams purer than 3.0 now make more FE per AP, and the heart crystal's purity raises the plant's output.
