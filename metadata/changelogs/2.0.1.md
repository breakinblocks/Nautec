### Added
- Combustion Dynamo: an engine that burns oil and water into 320 FE/t. Redstone in its additive slot raises that to 480 FE/t for a minute of burning, at 10% more fuel. JEI shows its fuel and additives.
- Lipophiles, a bacteria strain mutated from Halotrophs with Kelp Slurry, which produces Algal Lipid.
- Kelp Slurry and Algal Lipid. The Mixer makes Kelp Slurry from Kelp and Salt Water, and oil from Algal Lipid and Salt Water.
- NauTec oil counts as `c:oil`, so other mods' oil machines can use it and the Combustion Dynamo can burn their oil.

### Changed
- A laser beam stronger than a machine needs now speeds it up: four times the power runs it twice as fast, nine times three times as fast, with no upper limit. This covers the Mixer, Mutator, Incubator, both Bacterial Analyzers, Pressure Forge, Colony Replicator, Grafting Station, Augmentation Station, Submarine Dock charging, and the Bio Reactor and Industrial Bio Reactor, where it stacks with Speed Upgrades. Jade shows each machine's speed, and the guide's new Stronger Beams page explains it.
- Deep Sea Drains pump faster on a stronger beam, with no upper limit. Four times the beam pumps twice as much Salt Water, so 80 AP gives 1,000 mB a second and 2,000 AP gives 5,000 mB. Jade shows the current rate.
- Grafting Anchor now takes an End Crystal instead of an Anchor, since Anchors can't be crafted.
- Laser Junction now takes a Diamond instead of a Heart of the Sea.
- Uplink and Downlink Arrays only lose their sky to solid blocks. Glass, leaves and water above them are fine.
- Building a Gateway with a wrench in creative mode now makes a wild Gateway that needs no power.
- The Eye of the Sea finds NauTec Ruins and other structures with less strain on the server.

### Fixed
- Wrenching a Deep Sea Drain's middle wall on any face now sets its laser port facing outward, and wrenching it again fixes a port that was set on the wrong face. Before, a port set on the top left the drain with no power and the wall ignored the wrench after that.
- Sneak and right-click with a wrench now picks up a built Gateway, from the core or any ring segment, instead of opening its screen or switching the wrench's mode.
- The hint text on the Gateway Address screen no longer spills outside the window, and the last colour column fits inside its row.
- Bamboo, Cocoa Beans, Glow Berries, Sea Pickle and Sugar Cane Biome Tanks show their plant again instead of a purple and black missing texture.
- Glowing parts no longer flicker when using Iris with a shader pack: the Gateway ring, the Confined Spawner's rune band, the floor glow under Prismarine Crystals, the Lantern Jelly, Vent Crawler and Abyssal Maw, and the Atlantean Rifle's charging core.
