### Added
- EMI support (optional). Every NauTec recipe type, info page and machine shows up in EMI, the + button fills Mixer, Laser Crafting Matrix, Incubator, Mutator, Bio Reactor, Combustion Dynamo and Grafting Station recipes from your inventory, clicking a machine's progress bar opens its recipes, and items and fluids can be dragged from EMI into Distributor, Conduit Tap and Confined Spawner filters.

### Changed
- The Tidal Rotor recipe now takes an Aquarine Copper Ingot in the center instead of a Block of Copper.

### Fixed
- Fixed a crash when the Wave Jet, Atlantean Rifle, Laser Crafting Matrix or Resonant Vault was drawn, including in inventories and the EMI and JEI item lists.
