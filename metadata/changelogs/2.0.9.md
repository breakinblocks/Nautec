### Changed
- The Extradimensional Energy Storage screen is laid out again. The four port buttons sit around a map of the pad, north at the top, instead of over the title. The readout shows the tier, how full it is as a percentage, the stored FE on its own line, a larger fill bar, and FE in and out on separate lines.
- Resonance Node, Uplink Array and Extradimensional Energy Storage screens shrink to fit what they show, so a block with no network no longer has an empty half below the readout.
- The Colony Replicator shows a short status beside the Inventory label, such as "No template" or "Needs fodder". Hovering it shows the full message.

### Fixed
- Sneaking and right-clicking a Confined Spawner with an empty hand releases it even when your off hand is holding something. Before, the click did nothing unless both hands were empty.
- A Laser Junction placed by a command, a structure or the NauTec showcase was full of water and flooded the area around it.
- The Extradimensional Energy Storage screen's port buttons no longer cover its title, and its fill bar no longer covers the FE in and out line.
- The Colony Replicator's status no longer overlaps its title, and the mode button's label fits inside it.
- The empty Nautechnical Distributor's hint now wraps inside its box and says what to click.
- The Augmentation Station's "Load a part" hint no longer runs under the Apply button.
