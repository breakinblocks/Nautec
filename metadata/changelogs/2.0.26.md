### Changed

- The Sea Scout's camera sits higher in the cockpit, so the hull covers far less of the view and you can see much further below you without tilting.
- Holding free look in first person now fades the Sea Scout's hull, so you can look straight down through the floor. Set how solid it stays with the `submarineFreeLookHullOpacity` client config option.

### Fixed

- Fixed the Sea Scout's Impulse Laser missing whatever was under the crosshair. Both beams now leave the nose and meet on the crosshair, and small creatures are easier to hit.
- Fixed the Impulse Laser dealing about a quarter of its listed damage. Both beams now land every hit, four times a second.
- Fixed Prismarine Crystals doubling AP when one end fired into a Laser Junction or other laser block that wasn't set to take a beam from that side. That block now gets nothing, and the other end carries the full output. (#41)
