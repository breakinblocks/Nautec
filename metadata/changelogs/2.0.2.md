### Added
- Current Conduits: one cheap pipe that carries items, fluids and FE. Plain conduit has no block entity, so long runs cost nothing. Sneak-use the Aquarine Steel Wrench on a conduit to turn it into a Conduit Tap, which connects to every block it touches.
- Conduit Taps set each side to take from or put into its block, with separate redstone rules and filters for each direction, a priority, and Round Robin, Nearest First or Random sharing.
- Conduit Upgrades (Eddy, Surge, Riptide and Maelstrom) speed up every side of a tap, up to 64 items a tick, 1,000B/t and 5M FE/t.
- Filters and Intricate Filters open up to 27 item and 9 fluid filter slots per side, as whitelists or blacklists, set by clicking or by dragging from JEI.
- Aquarine Copper: fire a laser beam of purity 2.1 or more through Aquarine Copper Compound to get four ingots each. Clay Gaskets and Aquarine Copper blocks and nuggets come with it.
- Resonant Vault and Resonant Cistern: linked storage. Every Vault or Cistern set to the same channel shares one 27 slot inventory or one 16B tank, wherever it is.
- Pick a channel with a four colour code, like the Gateway, and set it to Private (only you), Team (your FTB team) or Public. Private and Team blocks can only be opened, changed or broken by their owners. A broken block keeps its channel.
- Resonant Expansions add 27 slots or 16B to a channel, up to four per channel, and work from any block on it.
- Each side of a Vault or Cistern can push into or pull from the block next to it, or be closed to pipes.
- Item transformation recipes can ask for a minimum beam AP, for modpacks that want a recipe to need a stronger laser.
- Laser Crafting Matrix: a machine that does every in-world laser transformation, like Aquarine Steel and Gears, so they can be automated with pipes. It takes a laser from the top only, holds up to 3 item and 2 fluid inputs and 3 item and 2 fluid outputs, and each recipe needs its own AP and purity. JEI and Jade show its recipes, needs and progress, and modpacks can add their own recipes.
- Starcatcher support: the Silt Skipper is a Starcatcher fish in NauTec's ocean biomes, and Starcatcher's warm ocean fish bite in the Prismarine Reef and Hydrothermal Vents.
- With Starcatcher installed, the NauTec Fishing Rod catches Starcatcher fish, with their sizes, weights and fishing guide entries. This can be turned off in the config.
- Starcatcher rods work in lucky fishing zones: the bobber stays in the zone, fish bite faster, and winning the minigame gives the same bonus loot as the NauTec Fishing Rod.

### Changed
- Winning the fishing minigame now reels in the catch for you, so there is no need to click again.

### Fixed
- The Incubator's feed slot now holds a full stack of 64 instead of 1, so it no longer clogs up and blocks Petri Dishes.
