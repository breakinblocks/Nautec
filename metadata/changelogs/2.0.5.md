### Added
- Resonance augment: install a Resonance Charm at the Augmentation Station to charge your items from a network without using a charm slot. It goes in the Head slot.
- Bubble Capsule: a single-use item that holds the sea back around your head for 30 seconds when you are running out of air.

### Changed
- The Colony Replicator takes the item its template's strain makes, or that item's storage block, as fodder, so a reactor's own output can pay for copies. Rarer items give more biomass (breakinblocks/Nautec#11).
- Colony Replicator copies start at a size of 10,000 instead of a fresh graft's size.
- The Incubator grows bigger colonies faster: each cycle adds 3% of the colony's size on top of its usual growth, so a colony reaches the size cap in minutes rather than hours.
- The Mutator can refine a colony's stats without changing its strain: use the storage block of the item it makes (or the item itself) as the catalyst.
- The Mutator has a booster slot for Electrolyte Algae Serum Vials. They make every attempt three times as likely to succeed and stop failed attempts from shrinking the colony. A vial is only used up on a success.
- Every strain with a nutrient now also eats the item it makes and that item's storage block, in the Incubator and in Bio Reactors. This includes strains for other mods' materials and custom ones. A block lasts nine times as long as the item, and the strain's own nutrient still works (breakinblocks/Nautec#10).

### Fixed
- Your head no longer spins twice as fast as the Sea Scout when you turn while piloting it.
- Bio Reactors no longer swap out a colony every time a new colony dish arrives. A full reactor now only swaps out Senescent colonies (breakinblocks/Nautec#7).
- A Prismarine Crystal now takes a beam into any of its six blocks, not only the core, so an Aquatic Catalyst no longer has to sit at exactly the core's height.
- The Resonance Node now sits centred in inventory slots instead of near the bottom.
- Sneak-clicking a Petri Dish onto a Bacterial Analyzer's input slot now sets a ghost input. An empty dish there asks the Distributor for any colony that still needs analysis. A slot that can never take the item now says so (breakinblocks/Nautec#12).
