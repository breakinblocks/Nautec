### Added
- Resonance augment: install a Resonance Charm at the Augmentation Station to charge your items from a network without using a charm slot. It goes in the Head slot.
- Bubble Capsule: a single-use item that holds the sea back around your head for 30 seconds when you are running out of air.

### Changed
- JEI's + button now fills the Mixer, Laser Crafting Matrix, Incubator, Mutator, Bio Reactors, Combustion Dynamo and Grafting Station from your inventory at the recipe's counts. Shift-click fills as many sets as fit.
- The Mixer shows its progress in a bar next to the whisk.
- Recipes take common tags (`c:`) for vanilla materials instead of the vanilla item alone. This covers slime balls, ingots, gems, redstone, ender pearls, string, gunpowder, glass, raw iron and their storage blocks, so other mods' versions work in the Mixer and the other machines and crafting recipes.
- Jade on a closed Deep Sea Drain now also says what would stop it pumping once opened, and the pump rate reads as what it would pump rather than what it is pumping.
- The Prismarine Reef now appears in temperate and warm shallow oceans, not only hot ones, so it is about twice as common and much closer to most spawns. The Bioluminescent Grove and Abyssal Trench cover more of the deep ocean too. This applies to newly generated chunks.
- Budding Prismarine now generates in the Prismarine Reef, in the top of the sea floor where you can see it, so the reef's Lithophiles graft can be done where it spawns. It is also more than twice as common under the Abyssal Trench and Bioluminescent Grove floors, and closer to the surface (2 to 10 blocks down). Vanilla Warm Oceans and Deep Lukewarm Oceans now have the odd Budding Prismarine in the top of the sea floor as well, and Lithophiles can be grafted from Budding Prismarine in any biome it generates in.
- Glow Grottos show up far more often in the Bioluminescent Grove: they get rolled twice as often, and a grotto that would break into a dry cave tries a few nearby spots instead of giving up.
- A full Diving Suit removes every underwater mining penalty: you mine as fast as on land, even while swimming.
- The Colony Replicator takes the item its template's strain makes, or that item's storage block, as fodder, so a reactor's own output can pay for copies. Rarer items give more biomass (breakinblocks/Nautec#11).
- Colony Replicator copies start at a size of 10,000 instead of a fresh graft's size.
- The Incubator grows bigger colonies faster: each cycle adds 3% of the colony's size on top of its usual growth, so a colony reaches the size cap in minutes rather than hours.
- The Mutator can refine a colony's stats without changing its strain: use the storage block of the item it makes (or the item itself) as the catalyst.
- The Mutator has a booster slot for Electrolyte Algae Serum Vials. They make every attempt three times as likely to succeed and stop failed attempts from shrinking the colony. A vial is only used up on a success.
- Every strain with a nutrient now also eats the item it makes and that item's storage block, in the Incubator and in Bio Reactors. This includes strains for other mods' materials and custom ones. A block lasts nine times as long as the item, and the strain's own nutrient still works (breakinblocks/Nautec#10).

### Fixed
- Budding Prismarine and the top of the Crystal Cradle no longer have a see-through hole that showed caves behind them.
- JEI lists a Petri Dish for every strain, including the ones added for other mods' materials such as Aluminophiles. Before, JEI merged every dish into one entry.
- JEI no longer says Halotrophs come from grafting air. Grafting pages for blocks with no item of their own now show the item you pick the block with, such as Deep Kelp.
- The Wave Jet, Aquarine tools and Batteries no longer replay the equip animation in your hand every time their power changes.
- The Mixer's input slots no longer cover its title.
- Your head no longer spins twice as fast as the Sea Scout when you turn while piloting it.
- Bio Reactors no longer swap out a colony every time a new colony dish arrives. A full reactor now only swaps out Senescent colonies (breakinblocks/Nautec#7).
- A Prismarine Crystal now takes a beam into any of its six blocks, not only the core, so an Aquatic Catalyst no longer has to sit at exactly the core's height.
- The Resonance Node now sits centred in inventory slots instead of near the bottom.
- Sneak-clicking a Petri Dish onto a Bacterial Analyzer's input slot now sets a ghost input. An empty dish there asks the Distributor for any colony that still needs analysis. A slot that can never take the item now says so (breakinblocks/Nautec#12).
- Conduit Taps can take items from and put items into any block a hopper can. Before, blocks that only had a vanilla inventory were skipped.
- Conduit Tap filters can sort Petri Dishes by strain. A colony dish put in a filter slot now matches dishes of that strain at any size, and an exact empty dish matches only empty dishes (breakinblocks/Nautec#13).
