### Changed
- New textures for EAS, Etching Acid and Experience Algae. Each fluid now has its own still and flowing animation, so pools no longer show a stretched diamond pattern.
- The Augmentation Station now says when a loaded part is the right item but not the version its augment takes, such as a part a modpack wants charged first, and names the version it needs. It used to show "Unknown combination".

### Fixed
- Bio Reactor progress bars no longer make large colonies look slower. A colony that finishes a cycle in under half a second now shows a full bar, and hovering a colony's progress bar shows how many items it makes per minute (the summary arrow shows the total). Larger colonies always produce faster ([#24](https://github.com/breakinblocks/Nautec/issues/24)).
- JEI and EMI Augmentation Station recipes now show the exact part a modpack's recipe asks for, with its name and glint, and that version gets its own entry in the item list.
