### Changed
- Confined Spawners put less load on servers and clients. The mob inside the cage is only drawn within 20 blocks.
- Conduit Taps are now weathered copper, and the connector on each block they touch shows its setting: orange for output, blue for input, purple for both.

### Fixed
- Laser beams routed in a loop back into a block they already passed through no longer multiply their AP. The beam stops before the block it would loop into.
