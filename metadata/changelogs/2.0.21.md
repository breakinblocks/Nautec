### Added

- Added a configurable serum chance to prevent each stat loss during mutation or refinement, defaulting to 50%.
- Added the Crystal Cultivator to accelerate budding crystals and harvest them with laser-powered Fortune, including GeoOre and AE2 support.
- Added consumable Mob Samplers for retargeting Confined Spawners, including modded mobs and capture-tag restrictions.
- Added Blaze Powder, Fiery Crystal, Crystalline and Stellar Combustion Boosters, reaching 3,200 FE/t with a one-hour Stellar booster. (#38)
- Added comparator output for Resonance Chamber inventory fullness. (#36)

- Fixed seams in flowing Electrolyte Algae Serum, Experience Algae and Etching Acid textures.

- Fixed Conduit Beacon status showing incorrect operating conditions. (#31)
- Fixed a crash when using a water bucket on the Deep Sea Drain. (#32)
- Fixed Aquatic Catalysts displaying zero purity on the client. (#34)
